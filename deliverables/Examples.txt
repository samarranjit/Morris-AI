PART IV: IMPROVED EVALUATION

Case 1
Input board: WxWWxWWWWWBBBBBBBBxxxxx
Phase: opening
Depth: 2
Baseline move: WWWWxWWWWWBBBBBBBxxxxxx
Baseline change: d0: x->W, b5: B->x
Baseline estimate: 1
Improved move: WxWWxWWWWWBBBBBBBxxxWxx
Improved change: b5: B->x, a6: x->W
Improved estimate: 162
Scores use different scales; compare choices, not their numerical magnitudes.
Improved immediate-board feature differences: {'pieces': 2, 'mills': 1, 'open_mills': 3, 'mobility': 2, 'blocked': 1, 'threats': 1, 'connections': 7}
Why preferred: Both choices capture a Black piece. The improved choice places at a6, which participates in three mill lines; the baseline places at d0, which participates in one. After the selected move the White-minus-Black mill-incidence count is 7 rather than 5. This gives more future mill connections. The decision also considers the opponent's reply at depth 2.

Case 2
Input board: xxxxxBxWWWWWBBBBxxxxxxx
Phase: midgame
Depth: 2
Baseline move: xxxxxBxWWWWxBBBBWxxxxxx
Baseline change: e3: W->x, e4: x->W
Baseline estimate: -13
Improved move: xxxxxBxWWxWWBBBBxWxxxxx
Improved change: b3: W->x, b5: x->W
Improved estimate: -45
Scores use different scales; compare choices, not their numerical magnitudes.
Improved immediate-board feature differences: {'pieces': 0, 'mills': 0, 'open_mills': 0, 'mobility': 3, 'blocked': -1, 'threats': 1, 'connections': -1}
Why preferred: The improved move slides b3 to b5, opening the existing a3-b3-c3 mill. It preserves the ability to slide back and reform that mill while increasing movement options. Its mobility difference is +3 rather than +1, its blocked-piece difference is -1 rather than 0, and its legal mill-threat difference is +1 rather than 0. The baseline instead slides e3 to e4. The new evaluator values active threats and freedom of movement as well as material. These are positional reasons, not a proof of a forced win.
