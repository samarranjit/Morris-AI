The handout estimator is piece difference in the opening, and in the
midgame/endgame it is 1000 times the piece difference minus the number of
Black moves, with 10000 / -10000 for the terminal cases.

The project handout lists optional features: piece difference, number of
moves, mobility, completed mills, potential mills, blocked opponent pieces,
and useful squares. Use the ones that have a reason in the game.
